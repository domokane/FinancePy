Class for managing the fixed leg of a swap. A fixed leg is a leg with a sequence of flows calculated according to an ISDA schedule and with a coupon that is fixed over the life of the swap.

These are generated immediately as they are for the entire life of the swap. Given a valuation date we can determine which cash flows are in the future and value the swap The schedule allows for a specified lag in the payment date Nothing is paid on the swap effective date and so the first payment date is the first actual payment date.
