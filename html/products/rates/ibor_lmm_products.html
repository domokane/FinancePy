Ibor Lmm Products

financepy.products.rates.ibor_lmm_products

This module implements the LMM in the spot measure. It combines both model and product specific code - I am not sure if it is better to separate these. At the moment this seems to work ok. THIS IS STILL IN PROTOPTYPE MODE. DO NOT USE.
