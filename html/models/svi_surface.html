Svi Surface

financepy.models.svi_surface

SVISurface

SVISurface(expiries=None, svi_parameters=None) -> None
Inherits from: ImpliedVolatilitySurface
SVI implied-volatility surface. The surface consists of one raw-SVI smile for each market expiry. Each smile is parameterized in forward log-moneyness k = log(K / F_T) using total implied variance w(k,T) = sigma_imp(k,T)^2 T. Between calibrated expiries, total variance is interpolated linearly in time.
